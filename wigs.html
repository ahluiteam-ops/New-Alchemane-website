Frequently asked questions

How long does a consultation take?+

Usually 30 to 45 minutes. We take time to understand your needs and answer your questions properly.

What happens in the consultation?+

We discuss your hair goals, look at the area you would like to address, and guide you towards the most suitable options.

Can I consult online as well as in person?+

Yes. You can meet us online or visit our Khar West studio—choose the option that feels easier for you.

Where is your hair extensions store located?+

We are on the 4th floor of Empressa Building, 2nd Road, Ram Krishna Nagar, Khar West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400052.

Will I be pressured to buy during the consultation?+

No. The consultation is for clear, honest advice so you can decide what feels right for you.

How do I choose the right wig?+

We help you choose a cap, length, texture and colour that suit your lifestyle and the look you want to achieve.

Can a wig be cut or styled?+

Yes. Human-hair wigs can be cut and styled to personalise the finish. We recommend having this done by a professional familiar with wigs.

How do I care for a human-hair wig?+

Gently detangle it before washing, use suitable shampoo and conditioner, and store it on a stand away from heat and direct sunlight.

Can I wear a wig every day?+

Yes, with the right fit and care routine. We can help you find an option that feels comfortable for your usual day.

Will you help me match the wig colour?+

Yes. Share daylight photos online or visit our studio and we will help you find the closest, most natural-looking match.

Tell us what you are looking for. Our hair experts will guide you to the right solution.
