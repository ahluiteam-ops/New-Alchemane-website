Your questions, answered

Is a wig the same as a topper?+

No. A wig covers your whole head. A topper covers part of it, like the parting or crown.

How do I choose the right one?+

We help you choose the size, cap, colour and fit. Visit our Mumbai studio or book an online consultation.

Can I wear it every day?+

Yes. Fitted and cared for correctly, a wig is comfortable enough for daily wear.
