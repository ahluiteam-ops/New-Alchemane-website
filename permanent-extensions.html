Our permanent hair extension methods

Tape-in extensions

  • Thin adhesive tabs sandwich a small section of your own hair.
  • The flat profile is ideal for blended length or volume.
  • They are professionally removed and re-taped as your hair grows.

Micro Ring extensions

  • A small silicone-lined ring secures an individual tip to your hair.
  • No glue or heat is used at the attachment point.
  • Ring size, section weight and placement are chosen in consultation.

Keratin Bond extensions

  • Individual extension strands are finished with a pre-formed keratin tip.
  • A temperature-controlled fusion tool forms each small bond.
  • Strand-by-strand placement gives precise control around partings.

Ice extensions

  • An extension strand is joined a short distance away from the root.
  • Adhesive is shaped into a compact capsule, then set with an activator.
  • The capsule size and placement are tailored to each hair section.

Can I go swimming with hair extensions?+

Yes, occasional swimming is possible. Wet the lengths with clean water first, secure them in a loose braid or ponytail, rinse immediately afterwards, and dry the roots and attachment areas thoroughly.

Can I wear hair extensions every day?+

Yes, when the method suits your natural hair and routine. Clip-ins should be removed at night, while professionally fitted extensions are intended for longer wear and need regular maintenance.

Are hair extensions permanent?+

No. Permanent hair extensions are actually semi-permanent — they usually stay in for 2–3 months, after which they're removed and can be reused.

How can I keep my extensions in good condition?+

Detangle gently from the ends upward, use products recommended for human-hair extensions, keep hot tools away from attachment points, and attend maintenance appointments on time.

Can hair extensions damage natural hair?+

They can cause damage if they are too heavy, fitted too tightly, poorly maintained, left in too long, or removed incorrectly. The right method, professional fitting and aftercare reduce that risk.
