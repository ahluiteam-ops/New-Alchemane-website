feel like the beginning.

Bright white. Confident serif typography. Beautiful hair photography. Four Pinterest references selected for specific lower-page layouts, with a clear way to make them feel like Alchemane.

Our visual anchor / existing hero

Air, light,
and beautiful hair.

Carry the hero’s white canvas, black type and photographic confidence down the page. Use generous space and fewer decorative elements. Let each section have its own composition within that same visual language.

01 / Favourites + the full range

A quieter product gallery.

Borrow: pale image fields, fine typography and category labels that create breathing room within the product grid.

For Alchemane: larger, consistently cropped hair portraits; short names and discreet details underneath. Keep filtering visible and quick-view controls accessible on touch.

02 / Founder story + testimonials + consultation

Let the words have presence.

Borrow: oversized editorial statements, small human portraits and very little surrounding decoration.

For Alchemane: a black serif founder statement on white, an authentic portrait, and one beautifully set client quote at a time. End with a generous consultation invitation. Use the hero’s palette.

03 / Craftsmanship + transformation + films

More variety in the photography.

Borrow: the changing rhythm of wide photographs, intimate details and asymmetric image groupings.

For Alchemane: one large hair portrait beside a smaller base or texture detail. Give transformation and film sections a larger visual stage, with captions kept outside the image. Keep the surrounding canvas white.

04 / Find your match + studio + hair guide

Layer photographs with restraint.

Borrow: the overlapping portrait and detail imagery, simple black calls to action and open spacing.

Recommended application / design proposal

One coherent homepage.

The strongest combination: reference 01 for shopping, 02 for typography, 03 for photographic pacing, and 04 for the studio and guidance sections.

Solutions & shopping

Give “Find your match” a large portrait and a short, clear set of choices. Follow with spacious favourites and a disciplined full-range grid. Unify image ratios, labels and controls.

Films & transformations

Use large stills with a clear play button and a short caption. Preserve the before/after interaction, but remove its heavy beige surround. Let images provide the section’s visual character.

Craft & founder

Introduce an asymmetric photograph-and-detail composition for craftsmanship, then an oversized founder statement on white. Replace the dark green panel with space, portraiture and a clear narrative.

Stories & studio

Set one client quote in large serif type beside a portrait. Present the studio with authentic interior or fitting photography, a concise address and one booking action. Real studio photography is needed to replace the current decorative arch.

Guide & questions

Replace the simulated 3D book with a photographic editorial card. Keep FAQs compact and readable with fine rules, generous tap targets and a simple open/close interaction.

Consultation & footer

Finish with a large typographic invitation and a visually quiet form on white. Use black buttons, understated field borders and a spacious footer aligned to the header.

Motion that supports the imagery.

Keep the hero’s cloud wipe as the signature transition. Below it, use subtle image reveals, restrained depth on larger photographs and gentle text entrances. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

A simpler visual system.

White canvas, near-black text, light grey dividers. Reuse the hero’s serif and one supporting sans serif. Remove numbered section labels, repeated brown italics and unnecessary surface decoration.
