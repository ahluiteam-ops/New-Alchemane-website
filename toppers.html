Frequently asked questions

How long does a consultation take?+

What happens in the consultation?+

What is a hair topper?+

A hair topper is a hairpiece for partial coverage, usually at the parting or crown. It blends with the hair you already have.

How do I choose the right base size?+

Measure the area you want to cover and compare it with the base dimensions on the product page. Use the measurement guide before choosing between 5 x 3 and 5 x 6 inches.

Should I choose a silk base or a skin base?+

Compare the base finish, coverage, parting and attachment details. The comparison above and the product close-ups can help you choose the option you prefer.

How do I choose my shade?+

Compare the product shades with your own hair in natural daylight. Contact our team if you need help with the shade before you order.

Can I style a human hair topper?+

Follow the styling instructions for your product, including any heat limits. Check with our team if you are unsure which tools or products to use.

Can I wash my topper?+

Follow the washing instructions supplied for your topper and its base. Use the recommended care products and handle the base gently.

Can I wear a clip-on topper every day?+

It can be worn and removed as needed. Make sure the fit feels comfortable, avoid pulling on your own hair and remove a clip-on topper before sleeping.

Can I have it cut or coloured?+

Cutting or colouring should be discussed with our team before you alter the topper. The right approach depends on the product and the finish you want.

Can I buy a topper online?+

Yes. Choose a topper, select the available options, add it to your cart and complete checkout.

What should I check about delivery and returns?+

Read Shipping and Delivery and Returns and Exchanges before ordering. Check the terms for your chosen product, especially if you request customisation.
