## Content and UX audit

### What is not working in the current draft and Figma

1. **It behaves like a landing page.** Repeated videos, repeated calls to action,
   a lead-magnet guide and sales-heavy comparison blocks make the page feel like
   a campaign rather than a permanent part of the website.
2. **The buying route is unclear.** Permanent extensions appear beside products
   that can be bought online, but permanent extensions require a consultation
   and professional fitting.
3. **The same message is repeated.** "Natural-looking", "fuller and longer" and
   "expert guidance" appear in several sections without adding new information.
4. **Some claims are too broad.** "Zero damage", "no glue", "no chemicals" and
   claims about unnamed competitors should not be used.
5. **Too many methods are hidden behind tabs.** The visitor should be able to see
   all six available methods before deciding to consult.
6. **The page asks for a booking before explaining the process.** A visitor needs
   to know that the consultation may be online, that it is paid and refundable,
   and that fitting happens only at Khar West.
7. **The proof is overextended.** Celebrity films are already used on the home
   page. This page should use verified client results, the Google rating and the
   studio's experience instead.

### Recommended content decisions

- Use one primary action throughout: **Book a consultation**.
- Place **Paid and refundable** next to the CTA, not inside an FAQ.
- Explain the complete journey near the top of the page.
- Show the six methods as an overview, but let the specialist recommend the
  right one. Do not make the visitor diagnose their own hair.
- Keep the Clip-in vs Permanent comparison only as a routing aid. Clip-ins,
  toppers and fringes can link to the online shop. Permanent extensions and wigs
  should link to consultation.
- Use one strong results section. Remove duplicate video sections.
- Remove the downloadable guide, celebrity section, competitor comparison and
  repeated promotional banners.
- Do not show product cards, prices, "Buy now" or "Add to cart" for permanent
  extensions.

### Recommended page order

1. Hero and consultation route
2. What permanent extensions are
3. How consultation and fitting work
4. Benefits
5. Six available methods
6. Real client results
7. Permanent vs clip-in comparison
8. Application and aftercare details

10. FAQs
11. Consultation form and closing CTA

## 2. What permanent extensions are

**Eyebrow:** Permanent, but not forever

**Headline:** Hair you wear every day, without clipping it in each morning.

**Body:** Permanent hair extensions are semi-permanent extensions fitted by a
professional and worn between maintenance appointments. They are blended into
your natural hair and can be washed, styled and cared for as part of your usual
routine. When your natural hair grows, the extensions need professional
maintenance, refitting or removal.

**Callout:** Permanent extensions are not sold through online checkout. Every
set begins with a consultation so we can recommend the right method, amount,
shade and placement for your hair.

## 5. Permanent extension methods

**Eyebrow:** Six professional methods

**Headline:** The right method depends on your hair.

**Intro:** We offer six permanent extension methods. You do not need to choose
one before booking. At your consultation, we look at your natural hair, scalp,
desired result and routine before recommending the safest, most suitable
option.

### Tape-in Hair Extensions

Flat extension sections are placed close to the head for an even, discreet
blend. A popular option for adding overall length and volume.

### Micro Ring Hair Extensions

Individual extension strands are fitted with small rings, allowing controlled
placement through the hair.

### Keratin Bond Hair Extensions

Individual bonded strands allow precise placement and natural movement through
the lengths.

### Feather Extensions

Fine extension strands are placed where targeted length, movement or volume is
needed.

### V-Light Extensions

A fine, discreet application method used where careful placement and a subtle
finish are important.

### Ice Extensions

A strand-based method designed for small attachment points, flexible placement
and a natural-looking fall.

**Note below the methods:** Not every method suits every head of hair. Our
specialist will explain the differences and recommend the method your natural
hair can comfortably support.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

### Can I buy permanent hair extensions online?

No. Permanent extensions are a consultation-led service, not an online
checkout product. Start with an online or in-person consultation. Your fitting
will take place at our Khar West studio.

### Can my consultation be online?

Yes. You can speak with our specialist on a video call and receive a personal
recommendation. The fitting itself takes place at our Khar West studio in
Mumbai.

### Is the consultation free?

The consultation is paid and refundable. You will see the fee and refund terms
before you confirm your booking.

### Which extension method is right for me?

That depends on your natural hair, scalp comfort, desired result, routine and
maintenance preferences. We assess these during your consultation before
recommending Tape-in, Micro Ring, Keratin Bond, Feather, V-Light or Ice
Extensions.

### Are permanent extensions actually permanent?

No. They are semi-permanent. They remain fitted between appointments and need
professional maintenance, refitting or removal as your natural hair grows.

### Can I wash, sleep and style my hair with extensions?

Yes, with the correct aftercare for your chosen method. We take you through the
routine after fitting so you know how to wash, sleep and style your hair.

### Will permanent extensions damage my hair?

Any extension method can place stress on natural hair if it is unsuitable,
poorly fitted or not maintained. We assess what your hair can comfortably
support, recommend the method accordingly and explain the aftercare you need.

### Are touch-ups free?

Yes. Touch-ups are free.

### Where are permanent extensions fitted?

All permanent extension fittings take place at our Khar West studio: 4th Floor,
Empressa Building, 2nd Road, Ram Krishna Nagar, Khar West, Mumbai 400052.

### I want something I can wear myself. What should I choose?

Shop our Clip-in Extensions for a removable option you can fit at home. Toppers
and fringes are also available through the online shop. Wigs, like permanent
extensions, begin with a consultation.
