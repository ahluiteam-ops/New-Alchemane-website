## Business direction

Hair toppers are purchased online. The customer journey is:

**Browse toppers > choose a product > select available options > add to cart > checkout.**

Permanent extensions and wigs remain consultation-led. The topper page has no
consultation CTA, booking form, appointment requirement, consultation fee or
consultation guarantee. Product support is optional and does not gate purchase.

## 8. How to wear and care for your topper

**Heading:** How to wear and care for your topper

**Paragraph:** Follow the guide for your topper's attachment method. For clip-on
toppers, start with these three steps.

1. **Position:** Follow the fitting guide to place your clip-on topper over the
   area you want to cover.
2. **Secure:** Close the clips into your own hair. The fit should feel
   comfortable, without pulling.
3. **Blend:** Gently blend the topper with your hair and adjust the parting.

**Video label:** How to wear a clip-on topper

**Care line:** Open the clips gently to remove it. Store it neatly and follow
the washing and heat-styling instructions for your product.

**Reviewer note:** Approve a product-specific wearing and care guide. Confirm
which online variants are supplied with clips. Do not present the clip-on guide

## 9. Frequently asked questions

### What is a hair topper?

A hair topper is a hairpiece for partial coverage, usually at the parting or
crown. It blends with the hair you already have.

### How do I choose the right base size?

Measure the area you want to cover and compare it with the base dimensions on
the product page. Use the measurement guide before choosing between 5 x 3 and
5 x 6 inches.

### Should I choose a silk base or a skin base?

Compare the base finish, coverage, parting and attachment details. The comparison
above and the product close-ups can help you choose the option you prefer.

### How do I choose my shade?

Compare the product shades with your own hair in natural daylight. Contact our
team if you need help with the shade before you order.

### Can I style a human hair topper?

Follow the styling instructions for your product, including any heat limits.
Check with our team if you are unsure which tools or products to use.

### Can I wash my topper?

Follow the washing instructions supplied for your topper and its base. Use the
recommended care products and handle the base gently.

### Can I wear a clip-on topper every day?

It can be worn and removed as needed. Make sure the fit feels comfortable,
avoid pulling on your own hair and remove a clip-on topper before sleeping.

### Can I have it cut or coloured?

Cutting or colouring should be discussed with our team before you alter the
topper. The right approach depends on the product and the finish you want.

### Can I buy a topper online?

Yes. Choose a topper, select the available options, add it to your cart and
complete checkout.

### What should I check about delivery and returns?

Read Shipping and Delivery and Returns and Exchanges before ordering. Check
the terms for your chosen product, especially if you request customisation.

**Reviewer note:** Confirm the final care guidance and policy links. The current
store contains conflicting processing/delivery timelines, and the landing page's
adjustment promise is not a clear returns policy. This draft deliberately makes
