# Wigs page: content plan

Copy and section plan for the dedicated Hair Wigs page (`/wigs.html`), updated
to the confirmed ten-section order.

This is a consultation-led service page, not a campaign landing page and not a
shop page. Wigs are not bought through online checkout. The page should help a
woman facing hair loss feel understood, see real results, understand the
options, and book a private consultation. Tone: calm, private, respectful, no
pressure, no urgency tricks. Mobile first; video and image led; one heading per
section and as little other text as possible.

**Sources reviewed:** Figma "Hair Wigs page-desktop v1" and "page-mobile v1"
(same sections, stacked), the Alchemane Wigs landing page, and the approved
Permanent Extensions page (for shared decisions).

## 6. Planning before chemotherapy

Heading: Planning your wig before chemotherapy (Figma)

Sub line: What you need to know before hair loss begins; so you feel ready, not
rushed.

1. **Plan at least 15 days in advance.** A customised wig takes 15 to 30 days
   to make. Starting early gives us the time to get everything right; length,
   texture, and colour without any stress.
2. **Your natural hair guides the wig.** Before treatment begins, we study your
   existing hair; its texture, density, and natural hairline. This helps us
   recreate a look that feels completely like you.
3. **Don't wait until after chemo starts.** After the first chemo session,
   roots begin to weaken. After the second, hair starts to fall. Your wig
   should be ready before your second session; so the transition is smooth and
   on your own terms.

Closing band: If you or someone you love is going through this, we are here to
help you plan with care and without rushing. All consultations are private and
handled with complete sensitivity.
