What’s the difference between a topper and extensions?+

A topper adds coverage at the parting or crown. Extensions add length or fullness through the lengths of your hair. The right choice depends on where you want the change.

How do I find the right colour?+

Start with a colour-match consultation. Show us your hair in natural light, or visit our Mumbai studio so we can compare shades and textures with you in person.

Can I take my extensions off at home?+

Clip-in, ponytail, and halo pieces are designed to be removable. Professionally fitted methods, including tape extensions, need a different fitting and removal routine. We’ll explain the method before you choose.

How should I care for my hair piece?+

Follow the care instructions for your particular piece. Ask us about brushing, washing, drying, storage, and heat styling during your fitting, as the right routine depends on the base and attachment method.

Can we have a consultation online?+

Yes. Choose “Online” in the form below and tell us your city and hair goals. The team can discuss the next steps with you on WhatsApp.
