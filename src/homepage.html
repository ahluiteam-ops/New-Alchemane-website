Need Help ↗
What is the difference between a topper and extensions?+

A topper covers thinning at the parting or crown. Extensions make your hair longer or fuller. We can help you choose the right one.

How do I choose the right colour?+

Send us photos of your hair in daylight, book an online consultation, or visit our Khar West studio. We will help you find the closest match.

Can I remove extensions at home?+

You can remove clip-in, ponytail and halo extensions at home. Permanent extensions must be removed by a trained professional.

How do I care for my hair piece?+

Care depends on the product. We will show you how to brush, wash, dry, store and style it.

Can I book an online consultation?+
